Daily Reports with Claude

How we build the morning Daily Report and the afternoon labor reports each day
Parallel runBuild it both ways for two weeks; switch once three days in a row tie out.
01
Summary
  • Morning: the Daily Report, its 4 PDFs and your morning email.
  • Afternoon: Claude builds each plant's Labor Shift Detail, the day's rows in your monthly Labor by Shift files, the Labor Detail workbook and the summary email; you review and approve.
  • Sources: 3 shift workbooks, 2 schedules, NetSuite, SafetyChain.
  • Your time goes to the judgment calls: off-schedule runs, count gaps, lunch rotations, and the wording of each shift's summary.
  • Nothing is saved until you say "approved"; the email then waits in your Drafts for you to send.

The review list
What each flag asks
FlagWhat it meansYour answer
Off scheduleHours or units far from the schedule, or an item run / skipped.Hours ran; scheduled units.
Unscheduled runA product ran that wasn't on the schedule; Claude shows the shift notes.How many hours it ran.
Made another dayUnits labeled or entered today for another day's production.Take them out of the day's hours and OEE, or not.
Count gapNetSuite and the supervisor's count differ by more than 5%.Keep NetSuite, or the corrected split.
Planned timeChangeover, cleaning or lunch was planned on the schedule.Downtime to take out, if any.
Units at 0 hours / late startNetSuite counted units into a shift that didn't run, or a shift started late with no downtime logged.Move the units or hours; count the lost time or not.
Training noteHC Notes name trainees but Training HC is blank.People and hours to book as training.
Lunch rotationsThe schedule's "LUNCH ROTATION" rows are booked already; Claude shows them and the notes that mention lunch.Crew and hours for the rest (e.g. Sollich-4's cleaning/lunch block).
Line cleaningCrew × hours the line didn't run; a crew that only cleaned is booked × 8 h.Correct crew and hours, or keep.
Downtime causeA reason Claude couldn't place in a Daily Report cause.Count it or not.
No standard crewAn item with no standard crew on file.The standard to use.
"Keep it" is always a fine answer. Every answer is saved with your note, so the next day starts from the same rules.

10
How it counts
Your rules, built in
  • Produced units: NetSuite completions by shift. L1 7:16 / 3:16 / 11:16, L2 6:16 / 2:16 / 10:16, L3 6:46 / 2:46 / 10:46.
  • Monday 1st shift also takes the hour before start.
  • Scheduled units and hours: the daily schedule; hours as scheduled (7.75 stays 7.75).
  • OEE goals: each line's standard for the year; a lower goal the scheduler writes in the schedule's Notes column is that day's goal and the run's labor standard. Plant goal: its standard, or the average of its running lines on a reduced day. CandyCo: 80%.
  • Matrix 1 & 2: one machine's pouch rate (large 34 a minute, small 30) × the machines that ran that shift.
  • CILs: 0.25 h per person on S1–S3, TR1, ML1, ML4; 0.5 h on ML2, ML3, FFS; only crews on lines that produced.
  • ML2, ML3, FFS: first 30 CIL minutes per shift are planned, not downtime.
  • Misc labor counts equally in standard and actual; rework (every shift) counts in actual only. Scrap comes from SafetyChain by line and shift, to the products that ran that shift.
  • Standards: your rate table. Approved exceptions: Sollich-4 at 75% for 2026; the FEA Heart crew at 16. New standards go to Scott.
  • Shift summaries: scrap achieved at or under 3%, HC at or under standard, labor at or under standard. Red and green in the files follow these rules on their own.
  • Prices: NetSuite sales orders. WIP is valued at what its line packed that day (the schedule's Pack rows).

Can't find the S DriveOpen OneDrive and make sure the S Drive is syncing; tell Claude where it is.

Push failedThe files are still saved. Forward the message to Scott (GitHub access).
EXCEL STEP FAILEDOpen the Daily Report, save it, and print the 1st, 2nd, 3rd Shift and Summary tabs to PDF. Claude drafts the email and logs it for Scott.
Anything elseTell Claude what went wrong or what you changed. It logs it for Scott, who is alerted the same day. Don't type numbers in by hand.
